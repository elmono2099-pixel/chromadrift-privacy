Privacy Policy for Chroma Drift

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Chroma Drift does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app has no internet access, and everything it saves stays on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Chroma Drift app ("Chroma Drift", "the app", "we", "us") handles information. Chroma Drift is published by Goosebumps Co. ("the developer"). By using Chroma Drift you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Chroma Drift does not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, sign-ins, or forms — we never ask for your name, email address, or any other personal details.

The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send any information off your device.

Information stored on your device

Your preferences — such as your chosen styles, colour palettes, brush, sound and graphics settings, and rock positions — are saved locally on your device only, using your device's standard app storage. This information never leaves your device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

If you use the Save button, the app saves a picture of your current creation as an image file on your device. That image is yours; we never see it or receive a copy.

Device features the app uses

  • Motion sensors (accelerometer and gyroscope), if your device has them, so you can tilt to pour and shake to stir. Sensor readings are used in real time on your device only. They are not recorded, stored, or transmitted.
  • Vibration, for optional haptic feedback.
  • Audio output, to play the app's sounds and music.

Information we do NOT collect or use

  • We do not collect personal, contact, health, or financial information.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, crash reporting, or advertising.
  • We do not include third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or files.
  • We do not make any network connections.

Purchases

Chroma Drift is a one-time paid app on Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store your payment details. There are no in-app purchases.

Children's privacy

Because Chroma Drift collects no personal data from anyone, it collects no personal data from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children (or anyone), in line with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the UK Age Appropriate Design Code (Children's Code), and the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Changes to this policy

If we add features in future that change how the app handles information, we will update this Privacy Policy, and the app's Google Play "Data safety" section, before those features are released. The "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
